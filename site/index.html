Smart Choice

Private Cabo transfers vs shared airport shuttle

Many first-time visitors debate between a shared shuttle and a private transfer. Here is the honest comparison:

  • Shared shuttles are cheaper per person but make multiple hotel stops, adding 30 to 90 minutes. You wait for other passengers and share luggage space.
  • Private transfers take you directly to your door. No stops, no waiting, no strangers. Your driver tracks your flight, greets you by name, and hands you a cold beer. For groups of 2+, the per-person cost is often comparable.

There is no Uber, Lyft, or ride-hailing at SJD Airport. Airport taxis are expensive and unregulated. A pre-booked private transfer is the safest, most reliable way to start your Cabo vacation.

A shared airport van is not a private Cabo shuttle. If you want a Cabo shuttle that leaves when you clear customs, book the private option.

Your Arrival

SJD Airport to Cabo San Lucas: what to expect

Los Cabos International Airport (SJD), also called Los Cabos Airport or SJD Airport, is about 30 minutes from San José del Cabo and 45 minutes from Cabo San Lucas via the toll highway. After landing, clear customs in Terminal 1 or Terminal 2, collect your luggage, and exit through the main doors. Ignore the timeshare vendors: your Cabo Transportation Concierge driver will be waiting just outside with a sign bearing your name.

The drive follows the scenic Transpeninsular Highway (Highway 1) along the Tourist Corridor, passing stunning views of the Sea of Cortez. Toll road fees are always included: you will never face a surprise charge. Most rides take 35 to 50 minutes depending on traffic and your hotel location.

Arrival at SJD

Immigration, customs and bags at Los Cabos Airport

International flights into Los Cabos International Airport (SJD) go through immigration, bag claim, then customs. Terminal 2 handles most U.S. and Canadian arrivals. Terminal 1 is used for some domestic and overflow flights. After you clear, both terminals exit toward the same ground-transportation curb.

Budget 20 to 45 minutes from seatbelt sign-off to the curb on a normal day; longer when several wide-body aircraft land together. We track the SJD flight number, not a clock time, so a delay does not cancel your Cabo airport shuttle. If you arrive at T1, follow the group-exit signs to the same outdoor meeting area. Do not pay anyone inside the hall for a “free shuttle.”

Once outside, walk past the taxi line and timeshare desks to Canopy 3 / Island Bar. The Cabo Transportation Concierge hostess holds an CABO TRANSPORTATION CONCIERGE sign. That is the start of your private Cabo airport transportation. Cabo airport arrival guide has the walking path on video.

At the curb

Timeshare vendors at Los Cabos Airport

The hall after customs at Los Cabos Airport is lined with people offering a “free ride” to your hotel. The ride is the hook for a timeshare presentation that can last hours. They are not airport staff and they are not your Cabo Transportation Concierge driver.

Keep walking. Do not stop to hear the pitch. Do not hand over your voucher or passport. Your Cabo airport shuttle is already paid (or set to pay the driver in cash USD). The driver will not ask you to attend a meeting. If someone says your reservation was cancelled or “upgraded,” that is a sales line: check WhatsApp +52 (624) 000 0000 if you want confirmation.

Taxi drivers outside are a separate issue: they are legal but unmetered. A pre-booked private Cabo transfer is the way to skip both the timeshare gauntlet and a curb negotiation.

Absolutely. We use real-time flight tracking for every reservation. If your flight is delayed, our driver adjusts automatically at no extra charge. You will never be left waiting or charged a late fee because of an airline delay.

Can I book transportation to Todos Santos or the East Cape?
Absolutely. We provide private transfers from SJD Airport to Todos Santos, La Paz, Los Barriles, East Cape, and the entire state of Baja California Sur. Popular East Cape destinations include Four Seasons Costa Palmas and Aman Nai. See long distance transfers to Todos Santos and La Paz for times, routes, and a vehicle quote.
Do you offer wedding transportation in Cabo?
Yes. We specialize in Cabo wedding transportation for groups of any size. We coordinate multi-vehicle logistics with SUV caravans, Sprinter vans for bridal parties, and limousines for the couple. Whether your venue is in Pedregal, Palmilla, Flora Farms, or a private beachfront villa, we handle every transfer with precision.
What vehicles are available for my Cabo transfer?
Our fleet includes Chevrolet Suburban SUVs (up to 6 passengers), full-size passenger vans (up to 10 passengers), Mercedes Sprinter vans (up to 14 passengers), Cadillac Escalade SUVs for premium transfers, and stretch limousines for special occasions. Every vehicle is late-model, fully insured, and equipped with air conditioning.
How do I pay for my Cabo airport shuttle?
You have two options: pay online with any major credit or debit card via Stripe (your rate is locked at booking), or pay in cash (USD) directly to your driver upon arrival. Paying in cash avoids the 16% tax, so your total is lower. Either way: no surprises.
Can I book a private driver for the day in Cabo?
Yes. Our Private Bilingual Driver service lets you hire a dedicated certified driver for a full day or half day. Explore Cabo San Lucas, San José del Cabo, Todos Santos, or anywhere in Baja California Sur on your own schedule. Your driver waits for you at every stop, perfect for shopping, restaurant hopping, or sightseeing.
What is the cancellation policy for Cabo airport transportation?
Cancellations must be made 72 hours in advance or no refund is provided. Changes are allowed up to 5 hours before the service. Departure-flight pickup changes need at least 4 hours notice. Full rules: Cabo transportation policies.
How much should I tip a Cabo transportation driver?
A tip is not required and is never added automatically. Travelers who tip typically leave 10 to 20 percent of the Cabo transfer in cash USD.
Can my Cabo airport shuttle stop at Costco, Walmart or La Comer?
Yes. Add a grocery stop at Costco, Walmart, La Comer, or Fresko when you book your Cabo airport shuttle so we assign a vehicle with cargo space.
Do you take extra luggage, golf clubs or surfboards on Cabo transfers?
Yes. Tell us at booking. We assign a Suburban or Sprinter with room for clubs, boards, and extra bags. No separate published surcharge: the correct vehicle is the solution.
Can I bring my pet on a Cabo airport transfer?
Yes. Cabo Transportation Concierge is pet friendly at no extra charge. Pets travel in a carrier for the full ride and we accept up to two pets per vehicle. Add the request when you book.
Is there a fee for pets on Cabo transportation?
No. There is no additional charge for pets on any Cabo airport transfer or local transportation service in Los Cabos. A carrier is required.
