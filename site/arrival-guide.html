At the Airport

What to do once my plane lands

Follow these simple steps to meet your Cabo Transportation Concierge hostess at Los Cabos International Airport (SJD).

1

Welcome to Cabo!

You are a few moments away from your airport transfer.

2

After luggage & customs

Once you have claimed your luggage and cleared customs, you will immediately see in front of you two doors that lead in the same direction. No matter which door you choose, you will pass many timeshare representatives who will try to make you stop or confuse you by saying they work for the airport or your transportation company; however this is not true and for your safety we highly recommend to avoid them.

3

Keep moving

Please continue walking without stopping until you have exited the airport.

4

Find your hostess

Once outside please walk past the taxi drivers and straight cross the yellow stripes towards the "Island Bar". A Cabo Transportation Concierge hostess holding an "CABO TRANSPORTATION CONCIERGE" sign will be waiting for you in Canopy 3.

Look for the checkpoint

A Cabo Transportation Concierge hostess holding an "CABO TRANSPORTATION CONCIERGE" sign will be waiting for you in Canopy 3, past the Island Bar.
